# Your team

[Sign in](https://app.superinsight.ai/app/login), then open **Team** to invite someone, or **Settings** for the compliance packet.

## Invite someone

1. Open **Team**.
2. Enter their work email.
3. Choose **attorney** or **staff**.

Adding people costs nothing. The invitation lasts 14 days. They sign in from the email. See [Sign in from an invitation](getting-started.md#sign-in-from-an-invitation).

**Attorneys** have full access, including the plan. **Staff** work on cases, records, and colleagues, and do not open the plan.

Referrals on the Dashboard are for attorneys at other firms. Someone at your firm is an invitation on **Team**, not a referral. They get 5 free tasks of their own.

To take an invitation back, choose **Withdraw** next to it. Their link stops working right away. You can invite them again any time.

When your firm’s email domain is verified, anyone at that domain can sign in and join without an invitation.

## Who is the admin

The admin manages the plan, the card, and invoices. On **Team**, the admin can choose **Make admin** on another attorney. You both get an email.

## The compliance packet

Records stay in your firm’s workspace.

1. Open **Settings**, then **Compliance**.
2. Sign the business associate agreement.
3. Download the countersigned copy.

You can open the same packet while you are starting a case, when the page offers it.
