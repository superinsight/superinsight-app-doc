# Cases and records

[Sign in](https://app.superinsight.ai/app/login), then open a case from **Cases** or the Dashboard. The tabs are **Overview**, **Records**, **Chronology**, **Tasks**, **Documents**, **Services**, and **Discussion**.

**Notes and Assist** sit on the right. Notes are yours alone. A colleague on the same case does not see them. Off a case, the panel is **Assist** only.

## Open a case

1. Choose **Open a case** on the Dashboard or on **Cases**.
2. Answer the short conversation. It starts with the client’s name. You can skip a question or stop and come back.
3. When a case with a similar name is already open, we suggest it. Choose **go to it** to open that one, or say it is a different matter to keep going.

**Try our batch upload**, on Open a case, takes a set of folders. Each folder becomes a case. You confirm the names, practice area, side, and files before anything is created.

## Overview

**Overview** shows where the case stands and the next step.

The **Case summary** holds the practice area, side, client, and overview. Edit any of them, then save.

**Rename** and **Delete** are at the top of the case page. Deleting hides the case from your workspace. If a referral invoice is already on the case, the desk still has that invoice.

**Notify**, at the top of the case, lists who at your firm gets this case’s mail, such as expert messages and chronology ready. Add or remove people there. Anyone you mention still hears about it.

When the case has charges, Overview shows them with the invoice, which you can print. See [The Superinsight invoice](tasks-and-plan.md#the-superinsight-invoice).

## Upload records

1. Open **Records**.
2. Drag in the file as it is: PDFs, images, or scanned faxes. No sorting or renaming. Uploading is free.
3. Records shows when the chronology will be ready, such as ready by a time on the clock. You can close the tab. We email you when it is ready.

Tasks open once the records are read and the chronology is ready.

## The chronology

Each row on **Chronology** is a dated event from the records: the date, the facility, the providers, and a quote from the chart with its page. Choose a citation to see the page itself.

When a record was signed, faxed, or printed on another day, those days sit under the care date on the same row. On a medical-malpractice chronology, the care date also shows the hour, minute, and second when the record printed a time.

**Full timeline** is always the first card. The other cards follow the case’s practice area, such as functional limits on a Social Security case or work status on a workers’ compensation case. **Not relevant** and any category you name sit with them. Choose a label on a row to filter to it.

**Filter**, above the events, opens a rail on the left. Narrow by provider, body site, dates, or abnormal results. Set a filter, then save it under a name. Everyone at your firm sees saved filters on this case, and you can copy a link to the one you are on.

Choose the pencil on a row to open **Edit event**. Change the day or file it under a category, then save. A date change leaves a **Modified** note in its comments. Filing an event as **Not relevant** takes it off the working chronology. You still find it on the **Not relevant** card.

**Click a citation** to open the source file on the right. With that file open, **highlight a passage** in the chronology event to mark the same words in the source. If the passage is not in the file you have open, but it is in another record, that record opens on its own tab.

Choose the comment icon on a row to post a note. You can edit your own comments. They go into exports with the event.

**Export**, above the events, downloads the chronology as Excel, CSV, PDF, or Word on a paid plan. Reading and filtering stay free. With a filter on, the export holds just those rows.

## Documents

**Documents** is where you keep a deposition outline, a demand letter, a client summary, or an answer you saved from Tasks.

1. Start one empty, or on a finished task choose **save to Documents**.
2. Every save is kept as a version. Open **History** to see them. Compare two versions, or restore an older one. Restoring saves it as the newest version.
3. Open the document to export it as PDF or Word, or to print it. The export notes how many pages were on file when you made it.

A deleted document can be recovered for 30 days.

## Discussion

**Discussion** is for your firm only. Experts do not see it. A post emails no one unless you mention someone, or everyone for the whole firm.

## Notes

On a case, the panel on the right is **Notes and Assist**.

- Type a note at the bottom, or choose **Record a voice note**.
- **Pin page** in the record viewer saves that page.
- Select text and choose **Highlight** to keep the words.
- If a voice note does not transcribe, the recording stays. **Try again** runs it again.
