# Getting started

Law firms [sign in](https://app.superinsight.ai/app/login) on a computer. Enter your work email. If that email is on Version 1 and Version 2, choose which one to open. You do not need a password.

After you are in, the header shows **Dashboard**, **Cases**, **Team**, **Support**, and **Settings**. An invitation email takes you to the attorney door instead of this shared page. Use the link in that email.

## Sign in from an invitation

1. Open the invitation email and use its link. It lands on **Attorney sign in**, or **Join** and your firm’s name.
2. Enter the **work email** the invitation was sent to and choose **Continue**.
3. On **Check your inbox**, enter the six-digit code or open the **Sign in** link in the same email. The subject is **Your Superinsight sign-in code**. Both expire in 15 minutes.
4. You land on the **Dashboard**.

A desk-placed referral can start from the payment email instead. After you pay, sign in with your work email and the case opens. Pay the referral from the payment link we emailed. See [Pay](experts-and-services.md#pay).

If the code expired, go back to sign in and ask for a new one.

## A personal email

Law firm access is by invitation, and the address has to be a work email. If you use a personal address, the page says **Invitation only**. We record a request for that address, and someone on our team gets back to you after reviewing it. An expert account is not created.

If you are an expert witness, choose **Join the expert network** on that screen. See [Getting started for experts](../experts/getting-started.md).

If you have no account yet and you are not using an invitation, the page says **No account yet** and offers **Join as an expert** or **Request attorney access**.

## An authenticator code

We ask for an authenticator code only when you sign in from a new device or a new location. A browser you have used from that place goes straight in.

If you do not have the app, enter a recovery code on the code screen. To turn this on, open **Settings**, then **Security**, and enroll an authenticator app. Save the recovery codes. They appear only once.

## The dashboard

The **Dashboard** is a quick look at the firm:

- **Needs you**, **Active cases**, **People in the firm**, and **Messages**
- **To do**, **Recent messages**, **People in the firm**, **Refer Superinsight to a peer**, and **Active cases**

**Open a case** is at the top right.

Each item under **To do** has an X to dismiss it. **Mark all read** clears mentions and chronology-ready news at once.

Sample cases are listed under **Active cases** with a **Sample** badge.

**Refer Superinsight to a peer** is for an attorney at another firm. You get 5 more free tasks for each peer who joins. Referring them does not add them to your cases. To bring in someone at your firm, invite them on **Team**. That costs nothing, and they get 5 free tasks of their own.

## Your name, role, and contact details

The first time you sign in, we ask who you are.

1. Enter your name when the page asks **What should we call you?**
2. Choose whether you are an attorney or staff, and the kinds of cases you work on. Choose **Continue**.
3. If your phone or mailing address is missing, add it when asked. A colleague’s saved address may be filled in. You can change it. These details go to an expert you hire, so they can invoice you and call if they need to.

Later, change your name, role, and practice areas under **Settings**, then **Account**. Change your phone and mailing address there too.

To change the sign-in email, open **Settings**, then **Account**, and enter the new address. We send a confirmation link to that inbox. The address changes after you open it.

## The sample case

You can try every tab on a sample case before you upload a client file. It is under **Active cases** on the Dashboard, marked **Sample**. It has synthetic records and a chronology already built. Tasks run on it the same way they run on your own case.

**Reset**, at the top of the sample case, replaces your copy with a fresh one, including tasks you ran and records you uploaded. It cannot be undone.

Cases, records, and the chronology are free. Tasks are counted. You start with 5 free tasks, and a colleague you invite gets 5 of their own. See [Tasks and your plan](tasks-and-plan.md).

## Request an expert from the public form

On the public site, **Request an expert** starts a case and tells us the expert you need.

1. Enter the **Case name** and the brief.
2. Sign in with the code we email. A PIN stays on that page so you can return to it.
3. We open the case on **Services** so you can finish the Expert Referral.

The price on that form is the referral fee. See [Experts and services](experts-and-services.md).

## Send records from an expert’s link

An expert you already work with can send a link that says **A secure way to send records** and **asked you for records**. The link has no patient details.

1. Open the link.
2. Sign in with your work email, or confirm with the PIN on the page.
3. On **Send records**, upload the rest of the file. It goes to that expert’s case.

## Contact an expert from their public page

If you wrote to an expert from their public page, the follow-up email says **Continue on Superinsight** and **Keep talking with** them **on the case**.

1. Sign in, or create your account, with the same email you wrote from.
2. On **Which case is this for?**, pick a case or open one. We add that expert so you can keep talking on the case.
3. Continue on the case **Services** card, then share records when you are ready. See [Message the expert](experts-and-services.md#message-the-expert).
