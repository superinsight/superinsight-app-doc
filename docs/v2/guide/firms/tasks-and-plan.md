# Tasks and your plan

Open a case after you [sign in](https://app.superinsight.ai/app/login), then use **Tasks** to ask the records a question. The plan and usage are under **Settings**, not in the header.

Cases, records, and the chronology are free. Tasks are what get counted.

## Ask the record a question

Tasks work once the records are in and read. Upload them on **Records**. **Tasks** opens when the chronology is ready.

1. Open the case and choose **Tasks**.
2. Type your question, or choose a suggested task. The suggested tasks are named for this file. Choosing one fills the box. Nothing runs until you run it.
3. Read the answer. It shows how the record was searched and cites the pages it used.

When the record does not have it, the answer shows what was searched, across how many pages, and which kinds of records are missing. Upload those records and run it again.

On a finished answer, choose **save to Documents**. It lands there as a cited document you can keep editing. See [Documents](cases-and-records.md#documents).

We also email named tasks for a case that has records and no task yet. **Run this task** in that mail opens the case with the question filled in.

## Free tasks, then a plan

The counter beside the box shows how many free tasks you have left. You start with 5, across every case. A colleague you invite gets 5 of their own.

After the free tasks, a plan keeps tasks going for the whole firm. Open **Settings**, then **Plan**, and choose the plan to move to. A plan adds a monthly pool of tasks shared by the firm.

On a paid plan, nothing stops when the monthly pool runs out. Each task after that is $4, charged each time it adds up to $100.

## Usage

Open **Settings**, then **Usage**. It lists every task this cycle with its date, its case, and who ran it.

## Card and invoices

Open **Settings**, then **Plan**.

- **Add a card** saves a card for later charges. Nothing is charged when you add it.
- **Invoices and card** opens your invoices, your card, and cancellation.

Attorneys manage the plan. Staff work on cases and do not open the plan. See [Your team](team.md).

## The Superinsight invoice

When a case has charges, **Overview** shows them with the invoice, which you can print. The invoice is billed to the law firm. It lists task charges and referral fees on that case. It is separate from an invoice the expert sends you for their own time. See [The expert’s retainer](experts-and-services.md#the-experts-retainer).
