# Experts and services

[Sign in](https://app.superinsight.ai/app/login), open the case, and choose **Services**. This is where you order a Case Evaluation or an Expert Referral, pay, and work with the expert.

You can order either one before any records are uploaded. Your order moves through **Submitted**, **Received**, **In progress**, and **Completed**. Full names and contact details come with the introduction, once the order is submitted.

## Case Evaluation or Expert Referral

**Case Evaluation** is a written report from an expert who can testify, answering your questions. The expert’s name is withheld. You download the report from **Services**.

1. On **Services**, start a Case Evaluation.
2. Pick the specialty. We draft the request and two questions for you to edit.
3. It usually costs $1,000 to $5,000. You are charged only once we find the expert and confirm the price.

**Expert Referral** finds an expert who can testify, for $500. The expert sets their own retainer and hourly rate.

1. Describe the expert you need.
2. Read the kinds of experts we found. Names are not shown yet. You see credentials and how they were checked.
3. Confirm payment.

A Case Evaluation gets you written answers, with the expert’s name withheld. An Expert Referral introduces an expert you retain directly, who can testify.

To ask for a different expert after one is introduced, tell us on **Support** or from the expert’s card. Reassignment on the same order is handled by the desk.

## Pay

Superinsight charges are paid by card, from the payment link in the email or from **Pay** on the case.

- A Case Evaluation is charged when we confirm the price, not when you first ask.
- An Expert Referral is the $500 fee. A desk-placed referral asks you to pay before the case work continues. After checkout you return to the case to share records.

The expert’s retainer is separate. It is not the $500, and it is not paid to Superinsight.

## The expert’s retainer

When the expert requests a retainer, it appears under **Invoices from the expert** on the expert’s card on **Services**, with who to pay.

1. Pay the expert by the instructions on that request. Online payment to the expert is not available yet. Superinsight does not store their bank details.
2. Choose **I've sent this payment**.

The expert marks it paid on their side when it arrives.

## Share records, or say no

**Give record access** on the expert’s card shares every record on the case, including later uploads. **Restrict records** takes that back.

If the expert emails you to ask for access, the mail opens a one-page yes or no. If you are signed out, confirm with the PIN, then allow or refuse. Refusing leaves the records unshared.

## Message the expert

Open the expert’s card on **Services**. The conversation is already open.

- Enter sends. Shift+Enter starts a new line.
- **Reply** quotes the message you are answering.
- The paperclip attaches a file that is not a case record. Case records are shared from **Record access** on the same card.

## When an expert leaves the file

**Terminate**, on the expert’s card, removes them from the file. The conversation stays. **Restore** gives them record access again.
