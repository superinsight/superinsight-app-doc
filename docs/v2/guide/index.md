# Superinsight Expert Witness

Superinsight Expert Witness is where expert witnesses work on cases with the law firms that retain them through Superinsight.

Experts use it in two places:

- **The Superinsight Expert app** for iPhone, iPad, and Android, to read the records firms share, add records to their own cases, upload their CV, fee schedule, and W-9, and sign the records-handling agreement.
- **app.superinsight.ai** on a computer, for the case Review and its report, record search and downloads, the Guide, and messages.
