# Superinsight Expert Witness

These guides are for expert witnesses and for the law firms that hire them.

On a computer, start at the [sign-in page](https://app.superinsight.ai/app/login). Enter the email on your account. If that email is on both Version 1 and Version 2, you choose which one to open. We email a six-digit code and a link. You do not need a password.

The Superinsight Expert app on iPhone, iPad, and Android is for reading records, adding records to your own cases, uploading a CV, fee schedule, and W-9, and signing the records-handling agreement. Case Evaluation, messages, and billing are on a computer. See [The phone app](experts/phone.md).

## For expert witnesses

- [Getting started](experts/getting-started.md): sign in, create an account, and build your profile from a CV, fee schedule, and W-9.
- [Your profile](experts/profile.md): keep your documents current, publish a public page, and read messages from that page.
- [Home](experts/home.md): the dashboard, referring a peer, and the sample case.
- [Cases](experts/cases.md): open a matter, upload records, ask an attorney for the file, and message them.
- [Case Evaluation](experts/case-evaluation.md): the six steps from the questions through the signed report.
- [Opportunities and billing](experts/opportunities-and-billing.md): answer an inquiry, accept an engagement, invoice, and buy tasks.
- [The phone app](experts/phone.md): sign in, unlock, and work with records on a phone.

## For law firms

If your firm was invited:

- [Getting started](firms/getting-started.md): accept an invitation, use the dashboard, and arrive from a public request, an expert’s records link, or an expert’s public page.
- [Cases and records](firms/cases-and-records.md): open a case, upload records, read the chronology, and keep documents.
- [Tasks and your plan](firms/tasks-and-plan.md): ask the record a question, and manage the plan, card, and usage.
- [Experts and services](firms/experts-and-services.md): order a Case Evaluation or an Expert Referral, pay, and share records.
- [Your team](firms/team.md): invite colleagues and sign the compliance packet.
