# The phone app

The Superinsight Expert app runs on iPhone, iPad, and Android. On a computer, [sign in](https://app.superinsight.ai/app/login). That page sends you to Version 1 or Version 2.

On the phone you can read records, add records to a case you opened, upload a CV, fee schedule, and W-9, and sign the records-handling agreement. Case Evaluation, messages, billing, and the inbox from your public page are on the computer. If a task has to be done there, the app tells you.

The tabs along the bottom are **Home**, **Cases**, and **Account**.

## Sign in and join

1. Open the app. Choose **Sign in**, or **Join as an expert** if you are new.
2. Enter your email. We send a six-digit code and a sign-in link, the same as on a computer. The subject is **Your Superinsight sign-in code**.
3. Enter the code, or open the link on this phone. Opening the link signs you in without typing the code.

A code works only on the device that asked for it, and only for a short time. If it expires or does not match, correct it on that screen or request another. **Sign in instead** returns you to the start if a saved session cannot be restored.

An attorney account is turned away. Law firm work is on a computer.

If two-factor authentication is on for your account, a new device asks for the authenticator code or a recovery code before home. A new expert is not required to enroll during join.

## Unlock the app

When you leave the app or switch away from it, the signed-in screens stay covered. Coming back asks you to unlock with Face ID, Touch ID, or your device passcode. The app only learns whether that succeeded.

## Update the app

If this build is too old to keep using, the screen says **Update required** and **Update to the latest version to continue.** Choose **Update**. An older build that still runs offers an update and lets you continue.

## Account, email, and sign out

**Account** shows who is signed in.

- **Change email** asks you to **Choose a new sign-in email**. The new inbox has to confirm before the address changes. **Use a different address** starts that step over.
- If your name is missing, home keeps asking until you enter it.
- Choose **Sign out** at the bottom of Account. If the account cannot load, sign out is still there.

A suspended account is stated on home before anything else. Email [help@superinsight.ai](mailto:help@superinsight.ai) if that is a mistake.

## Security

On **Account**, open **Security**.

1. Choose **Turn on two-factor authentication**.
2. Choose **Open in authenticator app**, or enter the key it shows.
3. On **Save your recovery codes**, choose **Copy all**, tick **I have saved these codes somewhere safe**, and choose **Done**. The codes are shown once.
4. **Generate new recovery codes** replaces the old ones. The screen warns you before it does that.
5. **Turn off two-factor authentication** tells you what you are turning off before it finishes.

A wrong authenticator code can be entered again on that screen. Choose **Cancel** to leave setup.

## Profile documents

On **Account**, upload or replace your CV, fee schedule, and W-9. If a file does not go through, choose **Try again**. The same documents appear on **Profile** on a computer. See [Your profile](profile.md).

## Records on your own case

1. Open **Cases** and choose the case.
2. If the records-handling agreement is not signed, choose **Sign the agreement** and type your name.
3. Add a record with **Choose a file** (a file already on this device) or **Take a photo** (one page of a paper record).
4. To ask the attorney for more, use **Ask the attorney to upload**. **Share link** or **Copy** puts the link on your clipboard. The confirmation is **Link copied**. **Turn off link** disables it. Files already sent stay on the case.

The link has no patient details. The attorney can send records. They cannot read the case from the link.

## Read records a firm shared

1. Open **Cases**. Every case you can open is listed.
2. Open the firm’s case and choose the record. Scroll and zoom as you would a PDF. **Go to page** jumps to a page number.
3. Leave and come back. The app returns you to the same place.
4. On the sample case, a chronology citation opens the cited page.

Opening a page is recorded the same way as on the computer, so the firm can see that the records were reviewed. If the firm takes access back, reading stops. Choose **Back to cases** or **Back to the case** to leave the viewer.

Messages that are waiting are listed on home and open on a computer. They are not answered in the app.
