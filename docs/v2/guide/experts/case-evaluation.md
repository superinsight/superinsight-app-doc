# Case Evaluation

Open the case and choose the **Evaluation** tab. If you are signed out, start at the [sign-in page](https://app.superinsight.ai/app/login).

A Case Evaluation is six steps: **Overview**, **Preparation**, **Review**, **Draft**, **Determination**, and **Report**. A step opens when the one before it is done. You can go back to any step you have reached. **Evaluation steps** lists them. The line under the title says which step you are on, such as **Step 1 of 6 · Overview**.

Superinsight builds the chronology and lists facts from the records, each with its page. Your responses, your determination, and the reason are yours. The report is put together from that work. You can edit its wording before you deliver it.

If Superinsight hired you for this evaluation, the firm does not see your name. The report shows your credential. You write to the desk on **Support**, not to the firm. What you are paid is set before you start, and it stays the same whether the determination is favorable or not.

**Save for later** keeps the step without moving on. **Continue** saves and moves ahead. **Saved** appears beside the buttons once it is kept. **Back** returns to the previous step. Overview saves as you change it.

If you still have an older evaluation letter, it appears as the **Evaluation letter** tab. New evaluations use the steps on this page.

## Overview

Overview confirms the questions this evaluation answers. The attorney, the desk, or you set them when the case was created.

1. Choose **Read the request** to see the firm’s own words. **Hide request** closes it.
2. To change a question, remove it and add the new wording. A question cannot be edited in place. Under **Add a question**, type the question as you will answer it, then choose **Add question**.
3. Choose **Show question history** to see every question added or removed, newest first, with who did it and when.

Changing the questions sends the evaluation back to Preparation, which builds a new chronology and new facts.

Overview continues once the case has at least one record. If it says there are no records, add them on **Records** or ask the attorney for them. On a Case Evaluation Superinsight placed, ask the desk on **Support**.

Choose **Continue** when the questions are the ones you will answer. Superinsight then prepares the evaluation from every record on the case.

## Preparation

Preparation is Superinsight’s step. From every record on the case it builds:

- **Chronology for these questions** — a table for these questions.
- **Facts for each question** — the facts that bear on each question, with their pages.

You can leave. The work continues, and we email you when both are done, with a link to Review. Review opens when both show **Done**.

If a part stops, choose **Try again** beside it. If the facts stopped because your tasks ran out, buy tasks, then choose **I've bought tasks — continue**. If it stops a second time, ask the desk on **Support**.

A record that is still being read waits. The facts start when it is ready.

## Review

Review is where you read the chronology and the facts, and search the records for anything else. It has three views: **Chronology**, **Facts**, and **Tasks**.

**Chronology.** Each row has the date, provider, facility, what happened, and the page. Choose a citation to open that page beside the review. Check anything you rely on against the record.

**Facts.** Choose a question on the left.

- **Keep** on a fact you rely on. Kept facts show under **Facts you kept**, and beside each response on Draft.
- **Set aside** takes a fact out of the list without deleting it. **Show set aside** lists them, and **Bring back** returns one.
- **Supports** or **Against** says whether the fact makes a yes to the question more or less likely. It is a label for sorting. The judgment is yours.
- Choose the source under a fact to open the record at that page.

Facts are what Superinsight listed. For anything else, search in **Tasks**, highlight what matters, or write it in **Notes**, then use it in your response on Draft.

**Tasks.** Type your question. It searches every record on the case, and each answer cites its pages. The line above the box says whether tasks are included on this review. Tasks on a review a firm hired you for are included. On a review you started, they use your own tasks. See [Task packages](opportunities-and-billing.md#task-packages).

**Pin page** and **Highlight** save to **Notes**. See [Notes, pins, and highlights](cases.md#notes-pins-and-highlights).

When records are added later, Review says so. Choose **Reprocess now**. You can also go back to Overview and continue again. That takes the new records in.

## Draft

Write the answer yourself, from your notes and the facts you kept. **Show the facts you kept**, under each response, lists them with their pages.

Choose **Dictate** under a question and speak. The words are written down as you say them. Choose **Stop dictating** when you are done, then read it over.

You need a response on every question before Draft will continue. If the records do not let you answer one, say that in the response.

## Determination

1. Choose **favorable**, **non-favorable**, or **undetermined**. Favorable means the case is favorable to the side that retained you. Non-favorable means it is not. Undetermined means the records do not let you say either way.
2. Explain why under **Why**.
3. Tick the statement, type your full name, and choose **Sign and write the report**.

Signing locks the evaluation and writes the report from your work. The fee does not change based on which determination you choose.

To change it before delivery, choose **Reopen to make changes**, edit, and sign again.

## Report

The report lists the records with the pages read, the questions, each of your responses, and your determination with its reason.

1. Read it through.
2. Choose **Edit wording** to change how it reads. **Source** shows the stored text. **Save wording** keeps the change. To change a response or the determination, choose **Reopen** and sign again.
3. Choose **PDF** or **Word** to download. Both use the Superinsight brief, with your name as initials and your credential.
4. Choose **Deliver report to the attorney** when you are ready. On a case you opened yourself, delivering marks the evaluation finished and nothing is sent to a firm. On a Case Evaluation for a firm, delivering sends the report and completes the job.

After delivery the evaluation cannot be reopened. You can still download it. The page says **Delivered — read only.**
