# Opportunities and billing

After you [sign in](https://app.superinsight.ai/app/login), use these steps to answer a case inquiry, accept an engagement, send an invoice, or buy tasks.

## Answer an inquiry

A match arrives as a case inquiry. Open it from home. The summary does not name the patient. The attorney does not see this thread.

1. Read **1. Case brief**.
2. Answer **2. Your fit**. Questions from the desk confirm subject-matter fit, availability, and engagement details. A required question is marked. You can **Skip** an optional one.
3. On **3. Review & consent**, read your answers. Tick **I confirm these answers are accurate.**
4. Choose **Send answers**. Nothing sends until you confirm.

**Answers sent — the desk reads every reply.** You can still **Edit** before you send. After you send, the response stays with the desk.

**Conversation with the desk** on the inquiry is private. The attorney never sees it. Write there and send. The same thread is available from **Support** in the header.

## Decline, or an inquiry that expired

Choose **Decline** on the inquiry. You can add an optional reason. Declining releases the case back to the desk. The attorney has not been introduced yet. The page thanks you for the quick answer.

An inquiry that sits unanswered expires. The page says **This inquiry expired.** New matches land on your dashboard.

On an engagement you were offered and do not want, choose **Decline engagement**. Confirm **Decline this engagement?**

## Accept an engagement

When you are selected, the inquiry becomes **Selected — engagement onboarding**.

1. Confirm the operational details: **Review / consultation rate**, **Deposition rate**, **Trial rate**, **Retainer**, **Preferred phone**, and **Mailing address**.
2. Choose how you want records: **Searchable PDF**, **Native files where available**, or **Paper copy**.
3. Confirm **My current W-9 is on file**, or choose **Upload W-9**.
4. Choose **Accept engagement**.

The case then opens with the records the firm shares, **Messages**, and **Billing**. Ask for the retainer from the case’s **Billing** tab.

## Bill on a case

**Billing** is on your own case, on a referral after you are retained, and on a Case Evaluation Superinsight hired you for once you are invited. It is not a separate item in the header. Open the case and choose **Billing**.

Firms pay you directly. Online payouts are not available yet.

1. Open **Settings**, then **Payments & invoicing**, and save who to pay and how. Home links there as **Invoice settings**. Letterhead and your default payment instructions live on that tab too.
2. On the case **Billing** tab, enter hours. Time captured while the case was open can be confirmed there. You can also add time by hand.
3. To ask for a retainer, enter the **Amount to request** and send it. The email to the firm includes who to pay and how.
4. Choose **Preview PDF** to read the invoice before it is numbered or sent. Choose **Send invoice** when it is right.
5. When the firm pays by check, wire, or another method outside Superinsight, choose **Mark paid** or **It arrived** on that payment. **Not received** clears one you recorded by mistake.

**Balance due** is your charges minus the retainer you still hold. If the retainer covers the invoice, no payment is needed.

On a Case Evaluation, tasks Superinsight included are not billed to the firm. You can include tasks you paid for yourself as a software expense. After the report is delivered, **Request payment** tells Superinsight how you want to be paid for that evaluation. **Print invoice** opens the PDF. Nothing is sent to the firm as your invoice on that path. The desk pays the evaluation. Ask on **Support** when you need the timing.

## Task packages

Cases and the chronology are free. Tasks are what count.

You start with 10 free tasks on your account. Tasks on a review a firm hired you for are included and do not spend those 10. Every other case, including one you opened, uses your tasks for the chronology, the facts, and each question you run.

1. Open **Settings**, then **Tasks**. The page is **Tasks on your account**.
2. **Usage** lists what has been charged. Hired work is marked waived.
3. Under **On-demand packages**, buy one. Pay once. The tasks never expire. A package also unlocks chronology export.
    - **50 tasks** — $500, $10 per task
    - **400 tasks** — $3,200, $8 per task
    - **1,000 tasks** — $4,000, $4 per task

If a chronology or a task stops because the tasks ran out, the case asks you to buy a package before it continues. The sample case does not spend the free grant.

Referring a peer who joins adds 5 free tasks. See [Home](home.md#refer-a-peer).
