# Home

After you [sign in](https://app.superinsight.ai/app/login), this is the first screen. In the header it is labeled **Dashboard**. The setup steps stay here until you confirm your profile. See [Getting started](getting-started.md).

## The tiles

After setup, home shows three tiles and a cases list:

- **Share with attorneys** — preview and publish your public page. See [Your profile](profile.md#publish-your-public-page).
- **Tasks** — how many tasks you have left, and a link to buy more. See [Opportunities and billing](opportunities-and-billing.md#task-packages).
- **Your profile** — the documents still needed, with a place to upload them.

**Get paid** sits above the tiles until payout instructions are set. Choose **Invoice settings**. That opens **Settings**, then **Payments & invoicing**. Firms pay you directly from the instructions you save there.

**Cases** lists every matter you are on. **View all** opens the full list. **Open a case** is the link when the list is empty. A case you have not opened yet can also appear here as an inquiry. Open it from the list.

If the profile is closed or suspended, home says so and points you to the desk. Contact [Support](../support.md).

## Refer a peer

**Refer Superinsight to a peer** is on home.

1. Enter their email under **Peer email**.
2. Choose **Send referral**.

You get 5 more free tasks when they join. Three referrals can be open at a time. If three are open, **Withdraw** one, or wait until a peer joins, before you send another. A referral does not share your cases with them.

## The sample case

The sample case is a practice file. It is not offered during setup. After you have a profile, open it from the link on home, or go to the sample from **Cases**.

The sample has **Chronology**, **Tasks**, and **Documents** tabs so you can see those tools. A real case does not. Those tools on a real case live inside **Evaluation**. The sample keeps no notes. **Assist** is on the sample. **Notes** is not.
