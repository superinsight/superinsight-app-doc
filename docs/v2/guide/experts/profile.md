# Your profile

Open **Profile** in the header after you [sign in](https://app.superinsight.ai/app/login). This is your CV, fee schedule, W-9, public page, and case history.

A firm, an attorney, or another expert cannot see these files until you publish a public page. You can replace the W-9. You cannot download it.

## Replace your CV, fee schedule, or W-9

1. Open **Profile**.
2. On the document you want to change, choose **Replace**, then **Choose file**.
3. Wait until the new file is read. Credentials and rates update from the file. You do not retype them.

A fee schedule you replace is the one later engagements use. The earlier file stays as the version that was on file when you uploaded it.

## Photo and bio

During setup you can **Add a profile photo** or choose **Skip for now**. Later, add or replace the photo and bio on **Profile**. Both are optional.

## Publish your public page

The public page is off until you turn it on. Sharing one document with one attorney is a different act. Publishing puts a page on the open web.

1. On home or on **Profile**, open **Share with attorneys**.
2. Choose **Preview your page** and read exactly what an attorney will see.
3. Turn **Show my rates** and **Show case history** on or off.
4. Choose **Publish my page**, then **Copy link**.

**See your page** opens the live page. **Unpublish public profile** takes it down. After you change the profile, choose **I updated my profile** so the public page matches.

You can publish once your name, CV, fee schedule, and W-9 are on file. Before that, the card says **Share later, if you want**.

## Case history

**Case history** on your profile lists past matters we read from a case log or a prior-testimony list. If it says **No past cases on file**, upload a case log or prior-testimony list on **Profile**. We pull the entries from the file.

**Show case history** on the public-page preview controls whether attorneys see that list. It stays off until you turn it on.

## Inbox from your public page

People who write from your public page land in **Inbox** in the header. This is not the record file on a case.

1. Open **Inbox** and choose the conversation.
2. Read the message and any **Files**. Choose **Download** to save a file they sent.
3. Write a reply and choose **Send reply**. They get an email that opens the conversation. They do not see your email address.

When that person opens a case with you, the thread is marked **On a case**. Further replies belong on the case. Choose **Open Messages on the case**. Files already in the inbox stay in the inbox. They do not move onto the case **Records** tab.
