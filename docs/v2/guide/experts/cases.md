# Cases

Choose **Cases** in the header after you [sign in](https://app.superinsight.ai/app/login). From here you open a matter, read records, and write to the attorney.

A real case shows the tabs that fit how you got on it. **Evaluation** is always there when you are reviewing. **Records** appears when you opened the case or the firm has shared a file. **Messages** is the attorney thread on a referral or a case you handed to a firm. **Support** is the desk, on a Case Evaluation Superinsight placed. **Billing** is on your own case, a referral, or a Case Evaluation Superinsight hired you for. **Chronology**, **Tasks**, and **Documents** appear on the sample case only. On a real case you read the chronology inside **Evaluation**.

An older evaluation, if you still have one, can show an **Evaluation letter** tab. New work uses **Evaluation**.

**Notes** sits on the right of every real case, beside **Assist**. **Assist** explains the page. It does not search the records and it does not write any part of your review.

## Open your own case

1. Choose **Cases**, then **New case**.
2. Enter the **Case name**, **Practice area**, and **Side**.
3. Under **Your workflow**, choose **Evaluation** if you are assessing the merits, **Testify** if you will write the report and testify, or **Not sure yet**. You can choose later on the **Billing** tab.
4. Under **How should records arrive?**, choose **I have the records — I will upload them** or **Ask the attorney to upload to my invite link**.
5. Choose **Create the case**.

You can paste the attorney’s questions, one per line, or choose **Not now** and add them on the **Evaluation** tab. You can also upload the request letter as a PDF or Word file.

## Who retained you

**Who retained you?** asks for the firm and the attorney: firm name, attorney name, email, and phone. Choose **Add the firm**, or **Skip**.

If you skip, the case shows as retained by an unknown firm. The firm and attorney print on the cover of your report. You can change them later on the case.

## The records-handling agreement

Sign before any page of a record moves. The case says **Records-handling agreement** and **Sign before any page moves.**

1. Read the statement: you upload only records already lawfully in your possession and agree to the records-handling terms.
2. Tick that statement.
3. Type your full name to sign.

On a phone the button is **Sign the agreement**. See [The phone app](phone.md#records-on-your-own-case).

## Upload records you already hold

On **Records**, add the file you already have. A record is read page by page after upload. Until the pages are ready it shows as still reading. You can close the page. Preparation in a Case Evaluation uses the record once its pages are ready.

## Ask the attorney for records

The link carries no patient details. The attorney can send files. They cannot read the case through the link.

1. On the case, when records are still missing, use the invite on **Records**.
2. Choose **Copy link** and send it to the attorney yourself.
3. If you lose control of a link, choose **Disable**. Nothing already uploaded moves. Create another link when you need one.

When the attorney has uploaded records but not shared them, the case shows **Request access from attorney**. That email asks them to let you see the file. On a case where the file has not arrived at all, the button is **Request records from attorney**. Their reply comes back on **Messages**.

On a Case Evaluation Superinsight placed, ask the desk on the **Support** tab instead of writing to the firm.

## Read a record

1. Open the case and choose **Records**.
2. Choose **View** on the file.
3. The page opens in the viewer. Choose a citation anywhere else on the case to open that same page.

The firm can see that a page was opened. If access is taken back, the viewer stops.

## Message the attorney

**Messages** is the conversation with the attorney on this case. The heading is **Conversation with** their name, or the firm.

1. Open **Messages**.
2. Write your reply and send it. Enter sends. They get an email that opens this conversation. They never see your email address.
3. To quote an earlier message, use **Reply**.

On a Case Evaluation Superinsight hired, there is no thread with the firm. Use the **Support** tab. The desk takes a question about the request to the firm.

## Notes, pins, and highlights

**Notes** is on the right of a real case.

- Type a note at the bottom, or record a voice note. Each one keeps the time. If a voice note does not transcribe, the audio stays. Choose **Try again** on the note.
- **Pin page**, on a fact or in the toolbar while a record is open, saves that page to Notes.
- Select text in the chronology, a fact, a Tasks answer, or a record page, then choose **Highlight**. The words are saved with where they came from.

A sample case and every page that is not a case show **Assist** only.

## When time pauses

With a case open, we log the time you spend there. If you stop moving the pointer, typing, or scrolling for a few minutes, the case locks and says **Time is paused**. Choose **Continue** to go back to the case.
