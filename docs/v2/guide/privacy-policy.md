# Superinsight Expert App Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 15, 2026**

This policy explains how the Superinsight Expert app for iPhone, iPad, and Android ("the app") handles your information. The app is provided by Superinsight, Inc. ("Superinsight," "we," "us," or "our"). This policy covers the app only.

The app is for expert witnesses who work with law firms through Superinsight.

## Information the app collects

The app sends the following to our servers, where we keep it. All of it is linked to your account, and we use it only to provide the service.

| Information | Why we need it |
| --- | --- |
| Your email address | To sign you in with a six-digit code and to send you notices about your account. |
| Your name | To show who you are on your cases, and as your signature when you sign the records-handling agreement. |
| Your CV, fee schedule, and W-9 | To build and verify your expert profile. The W-9 includes your taxpayer identification number, which is needed for tax reporting when you are paid. |
| Records you add to your own cases | When you choose a file or take a photo of a paper record and add it to one of your cases, so we can prepare it for your review. |
| Your reading activity | When you open a page of a record, we note it, and while a page is on screen the app reports that it is open. This lets the law firm see that its records were reviewed. |
| An installation ID | A random identifier the app creates when it is first opened, so that a sign-in code only works on the device that asked for it. It is not your device's advertising identifier or a hardware identifier. |

With each request, the app also tells our servers its name, version, and build number, and whether your device runs iOS or Android, so we can tell you when the app needs updating.

## What the app does not do

- It does not track you. We don't combine your information with data from other companies, and we don't share it with advertisers or data brokers.
- It has no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting, and no third-party software that sends information about you anywhere.
- Face ID, Touch ID, and your device passcode are handled by your device. The app only learns whether unlocking succeeded.
- It does not save the record pages you read on your device. They are shown, not stored.
- It uses the camera and your photo library only when you choose to take or pick a photo to upload.
- We don't sell your information.

## How we share information

We share this information only with the law firms on your cases and with service providers that host, email, and process it for us, including AI services that read the documents you upload. They may use it only to provide those services and must protect it as this policy does.

We may also disclose information when the law requires it, such as in response to a valid legal process.

## Health information and HIPAA

The records on your cases can contain protected health information. Superinsight handles them as required by the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) and under the business associate agreements it signs with the law firms that share them. Before you add records to your own cases, you sign our records-handling agreement in the app, which sets out how we protect them.

## How we protect information

- Your information is encrypted in transit and at rest, and stored in the United States.
- Sign-in uses a six-digit code sent to your email, and a code only works on the device that requested it. You can also turn on two-factor authentication in Account, then Security.
- The app locks after 30 seconds in the background and hides your cases in the app switcher.
- Photos and files you pick are removed from the app's temporary storage after they upload, and signing out clears what the app kept for your account.

## How long we keep information

We keep the information described above for as long as your account is open.

Our system logs record identifiers, never the contents of your records, and expire after 90 days.

When you ask us to close your account or delete your data, we follow the steps in the next section.

## Closing your account and deleting your data

To close your account or have your data deleted, email [help@superinsight.ai](mailto:help@superinsight.ai) from the email address on your account. Closing your account and deleting your data are the same request.

We reply to confirm we have your request. We act only on requests sent from the email address on the account.

Within 30 days, we:

- close your account and end its sign-in sessions;
- delete your CV, fee schedule, and W-9, unless an exception below applies;
- delete your own cases and the records on them, including records an attorney added through your upload link.

We keep:

- your name and email address on the closed account, so we don't contact you again and can reopen the account if you ask;
- records on a law firm's cases, which belong to the firm, and the reports prepared for law firms, which are the attorneys' work product;
- your reading activity on a law firm's cases, which is part of the firm's record of its case;
- your W-9, if Superinsight has paid you, for as long as tax law requires, and then we delete it;
- records of invoices and payments, for as long as tax and accounting law requires.

Copies in our encrypted backups expire on their own within 90 days.

When we finish, we email you to confirm, listing what we deleted and what we kept. If you change your mind later, we can reopen your account, but files we have deleted can't be recovered.

## Your choices

- You can update your sign-in email address in the app, in Account, then Change email.
- You can replace your CV, fee schedule, and W-9 at any time in Account, then Profile documents.
- You can turn off the app's access to your camera and photos in your device's Settings.
- To ask for a copy of your information or to correct it, email [help@superinsight.ai](mailto:help@superinsight.ai).

## Children

The app is for professionals and is not directed to children. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has given us information, email [help@superinsight.ai](mailto:help@superinsight.ai) and we will delete it.
