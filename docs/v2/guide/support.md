# Support

Help for expert witnesses using the Superinsight Expert app and app.superinsight.ai.

## Contact us

Email [help@superinsight.ai](mailto:help@superinsight.ai) from the email address on your account. Tell us what you were trying to do and what happened. Please leave out patient names and other details from the records.

On a computer, you can also choose **Support** in the header of app.superinsight.ai to write to the Superinsight desk.

## Signing in

There is no password. Enter the email address on your Superinsight account and tap **Send code**. We email you a six-digit code; enter it to sign in.

If the code doesn't arrive:

- Check that you entered the email address on your account.
- Look in your spam or junk folder.
- A code works only on the device that asked for it, and only for a short time. If it has expired, request a new one.

If your account has two-factor authentication turned on, the app also asks for a code from your authenticator app or one of your recovery codes.

## What stays on a computer

The app is for reading records, adding records to your own cases, uploading your profile documents, and signing the records-handling agreement. The case Review and its report, record search and downloads, the Guide, and messages are on app.superinsight.ai on a computer.

## Closing your account or deleting your data

Email [help@superinsight.ai](mailto:help@superinsight.ai) from the email address on your account. The [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md#closing-your-account-and-deleting-your-data) explains what we delete, what we keep, and how long it takes.
