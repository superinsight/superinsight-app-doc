# Support

Email us, or write from inside Superinsight after you [sign in](https://app.superinsight.ai/app/login). That page sends you to Version 1 or Version 2.

## Contact us

Email [help@superinsight.ai](mailto:help@superinsight.ai) from the email on your account. Tell us what you were trying to do and what happened. Please leave out patient names and anything else from the records.

## Write to us from Superinsight

Experts: choose **Support** in the header. That message goes to the Superinsight desk. On a Case Evaluation, use the **Support** tab on the case instead, so the note stays with that case. The firm cannot see it, and the desk cannot see your review. If the question is about what the firm asked for, the desk takes it to the firm.

Law firms: **Support** in the header has two tabs. **AI Assist** answers questions about how Superinsight works. **Help Desk** writes to our team. Only you can see those messages. The **Assist** panel on the right of a page is separate. It only talks about the screen you have open.

## Signing in

You do not need a password. [Sign in](https://app.superinsight.ai/app/login) and enter your email. If the address is on Version 1 and Version 2, pick one before a code is sent.

- Expert witnesses use **Create account** or **Sign in** on the expert door. See [Getting started](experts/getting-started.md#sign-in).
- People at a law firm use the invitation email and a work address. See [Getting started](firms/getting-started.md#sign-in-from-an-invitation).

We email a six-digit code and a sign-in link. The subject is **Your Superinsight sign-in code**. Both expire in 15 minutes and work only for that request.

If the code does not arrive:

1. Check that you entered the email on your account.
2. Look in spam or promotions.
3. Choose **Send another code**, or go back and request a new one.
4. Choose **Use a different email** if you typed the wrong address.

If your account has two-factor authentication turned on, a new device or a new location also asks for a code from your authenticator app, or one of your recovery codes.

## What stays on a computer

The Superinsight Expert app is for reading records, adding records to your own cases, uploading your profile documents, and signing the records-handling agreement. Case Evaluation, messages with an attorney or the desk, and billing are on a computer. See [The phone app](experts/phone.md).

## Closing your account or deleting your data

Email [help@superinsight.ai](mailto:help@superinsight.ai) from the email address on your account. The [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md#closing-your-account-and-deleting-your-data) explains what we delete, what we keep, and how long it takes.
