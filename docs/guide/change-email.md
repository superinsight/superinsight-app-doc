# Change Your Email

Change the email on your Superinsight account from **Settings**. Superinsight emails a verification code to the new address. The new email is saved after you verify it.

Use this page when you want to change the email on your account, update your email address, or confirm a new email.

## Steps

1. Open Superinsight and click **Settings** in the left sidebar.
2. Under **Account Information**, find **Email**.
3. Click **Change**.
4. Under **New Email**, enter the new email address.
5. Click **Send Code**. Superinsight shows **Email Verification has been sent**, and the button changes to **Sent**.
6. Open the new inbox and copy the verification code.
7. Enter the code in **Enter verification code**.
8. Click **Verify**.

!!! success "You're done when…"
    Superinsight shows **Your Email has been verified** and returns to **Account Information** with the new email marked **Verified**.

## The verification email did not arrive

1. Check the spelling of the new address.
2. Check the spam or junk folder for that inbox.
3. Go back to **Settings**, open **Change** again, and click **Send Code** with the correct address.
