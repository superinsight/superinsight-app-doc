# Log Out

Log out of Superinsight from **Settings**. This signs you out of the app on this device. Your cases, reports, and account stay in place.

Use this page when you want to log out, sign out, or switch to a different account.

## Steps

1. Open Superinsight and click **Settings** in the left sidebar.
2. Click **Log out**.

!!! success "You're done when…"
    Superinsight returns to the sign-in screen. To open the account again, [sign in](sign-in.md) with the mobile number on the account.
