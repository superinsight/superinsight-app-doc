# Reactivate Your Subscription

Turn a scheduled cancellation back on, or start a plan again after it has ended. Only the organization owner can do this. Open **Subscription** in the left sidebar and select the organization first if you belong to more than one.

Use this page to reactivate my subscription, undo a cancel, resubscribe, or keep the current plan.

## While a cancellation is scheduled

The subscription status shows **Cancellation scheduled** and the end date. The plan button on the card says **REACTIVATE**.

1. Click **REACTIVATE**.
2. In **Continue your subscription**, click **Reactivate** to keep the current plan. Or click **View other plans** to choose a different plan.
3. In **Reactivate subscription**, click **Reactivate**. Click **Go back** if you want to stay on the previous screen.

!!! success "You're done when…"
    Superinsight shows **Your subscription has been reactivated.** The plan continues and renews at the end of the current billing period.

## After the subscription has ended

The plan button says **RESUBSCRIBE**.

1. Click **RESUBSCRIBE**.
2. Choose a plan and complete checkout.

## When there is no plan yet

The plan button says **SUBSCRIBE**. Click it and choose a plan.

An active plan shows **UPGRADE** when you want a different credit tier. See [Manage subscription](subscription.md).
